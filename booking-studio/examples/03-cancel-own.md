# Example 3: a member cancels their own booking

Message: "Greta Voss (m-110): please cancel my Tuesday Yin Yoga."

Starting state: Greta holds a booked reservation in Yin Yoga.

Good behaviour: the agent finds Greta's reservations, cancels the Yin Yoga one and replies that the place is free again.

Final state: Greta's Yin Yoga reservation is cancelled, and no other reservation changed.

# Example 1: a booking with room

Message: "Hi, Sol Ferreira (m-104) here. Please book me into Saturday's Beginner Basics."

Starting state: Beginner Basics has capacity 10 and 3 booked places; Sol holds no reservation in it.

Good behaviour: the agent looks up the class and Sol's reservations, books Sol and replies "You're booked into Beginner Basics, Saturday 10:00."

Final state: Sol holds one booked reservation in Beginner Basics, which is now at 4 of 10.
